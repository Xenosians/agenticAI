Only when the final user explicitly requests a result-dependent operation,
emit an earlier unconditional read followed by a conditional requested mutation.
The mutation has a sibling field (outside intent):
"when":{"source_agent":"<earlier agent>","result_field":"<source capability condition_fields entry>","equals":"<requested scalar>"}.
equals may be a string, number, boolean or null, never an object, list or
expression. The source is unconditional and read-only; the target is a mutation.
Never invent fields, evaluate conditions, authorize execution or chain conditions.
At most one conditional mutation per specialist. All ordinary grounding rules
still apply. Without an explicit user condition, omit when entirely.
